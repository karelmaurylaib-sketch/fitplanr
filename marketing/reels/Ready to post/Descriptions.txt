==================== TIPS 1 ====================
Only one pair of dumbbells? You can still get stronger. Save this 📌

1. Add reps before weight: climb from 8 to 12.
2. Hit 12 on every set? Add a set.
3. Maxed out? Slow it down: 3 seconds down, 1 pause, 1 up.

FitPlanr does this math for you every session. Link in bio, or comment FIT and I'll send it to you.

How long should you rest between sets? Save this 📌

• Big moves (squats, presses, rows): 90 seconds
• Small moves (curls, raises): 60 seconds
• Heavy sets of 5–8 for strength: 2.5 minutes

How heavy should you start? Save this 📌

1. Pick a weight you could lift 2–3 more times, then stop.
2. Week 1 is for learning the moves, not testing your max.
3. Add reps every week. Only then, add weight.
