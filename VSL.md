# FitPlanr: 60-second film, voice-over script

The landing page plays this film as motion design (HTML/CSS, no video file, muted, with a Replay button).
To use a real video instead, record the voice-over below over a screen capture and save it as `public/vsl.mp4`:
the page switches to the video automatically.

| # | Scene (on screen) | Time | Voice-over (calm, confident, US English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The problem: saved-workout chips float everywhere | 0:00–0:08 | "You've got two dumbbells. And about four hundred saved workout videos." |
| 2 | The problem: dumbbell wobbles, stopwatch spins, a big "?" | 0:08–0:17 | "Every session, it's the same thing: guess the moves, guess the weight, guess how long to rest." |
| 3 | The click: "2 dumbbells" lights up lime | 0:17–0:25 | "What if your plan started from what you actually own?" |
| 4 | FitPlanr in action: gear chips toggle on | 0:25–0:34 | "With FitPlanr, you pick your gear and answer five quick taps: your goal, your level, your days." |
| 5 | The result: four week cards build themselves | 0:34–0:42 | "Sixty seconds later, your four-week plan is ready. Every exercise uses only your gear." |
| 6 | What changes: bars climb from 3×8 to 4×12 | 0:42–0:51 | "Week one, twenty-five pounds for eight. By week four, more reps, more sets. Same dumbbells. Real progress." |
| 7 | Call to action: the FitPlanr logo, "Build my plan" | 0:51–1:00 | "Stop guessing. Start tonight. Tap 'Build my plan'. It's free to try." |

Rules: no fake numbers, no before/after body claims, no medical promises.
